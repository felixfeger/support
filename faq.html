Frequently asked questions

How do I pay my fare?

Tap a BrickPass card, digital wallet, credit, or debit card on the validator when you board. Fares are deducted from your card balance.

How do I add money to my card?

If using BrickPass cards, visit mybrickpass.citymetro.xyz to manage your card. Updated balances show the next time you tap.

My card was declined. What should I do?

Check your balance first. If it is sufficient, hold the card flat against the validator for two seconds, then contact customer or talk to any City Metro employee.

Where can I see delays and closures?

Live service alerts are posted at alerts.citymetro.xyz.

I lost something on a train. How do I get it back?

Contact Lost and Found through the Support Center with the line, time and a description of the item.

Are stations and vehicles accessible?

Yes. Ask a station attendant or the support assistant for step-free routes and accessibility help.

How do I report a problem or give feedback?

Use the support assistant or contact Customer Support from the contact page.
